# How to get out of the game?

Some people are just out of the game. They can do anything that they want. The societal and market pressure of earning money makes me wanna use AI for solving all my problems even though it doesn't make me that fulfilled .. but I don't have anytother option. But people like George Hotz, Andrej Karaptathy, Vitalik Buterin have all escaped the game. They are so good at what they do that they can do whatever they like .. however they like. So how did they do it. 

And more importantly. What defines this: "escaped out of reality person"

A couple of things:

1. They are extremely revered by the technological community i.e they are highly employable. All managers wanna have them. 
2. They are extremely rich so they don't really need to work for anyone. 
 
This makes me wonder whether you can really be like them if you keep using AI to outsource your thinking and doing because these guys became the way they are because they worked hard in the trenches. 

But "trenches" is a relative term. Each time it changes. People before vitalik, andrej and george and mark zuckerberg were in the trenches according to them and they "had it tough". Therefore they could've argued that THAT generation cannot be great because they have it all so easy. But I guess the goal post, the hard things, the techinicalities and the difficulties keep changing. But yeah AI is different. It has the ability to completely eradicate all the aforementioned difficulties.... which would leave humans with nothing to do except eat food and entertained .. but till then .... the goal post has moved. Currently it is ML engineering, Model tranining, GPU inference, World Models, Reinforcement Learning, Robotics and all of those things.
